Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about scam detection, response, and safety.

What should I do if I think my banking credentials were leaked? expand_more
Stop interacting with the suspicious message or website. Contact your bank through an independently verified official channel, secure affected accounts, and report financial cyber fraud through the appropriate official reporting route.
How should I handle sensitive screenshots or messages? expand_more
Only submit information necessary for analysis. Avoid exposing passwords, OTPs, full card numbers, PINs, or other unnecessary sensitive information. ScamSense's current scanner pages send the submitted content to the configured analysis backend for processing.
How can I recognize a suspicious phishing link? expand_more
Watch for unusual domains, suspicious URL structures, shortened links, IP addresses instead of domains, urgency, and requests for passwords, OTPs, or payment details. You can paste the URL into the ScamSense URL Scanner before opening it.
Is it safe to scan an unknown QR code? expand_more
Do not automatically trust an unknown QR code. Use ScamSense's QR Code Safe page to decode the content first. For UPI payment QR codes, always verify the recipient and payment details before approving a transaction.
