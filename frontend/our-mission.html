Our Principles

How We Approach Digital Safety

visibility

Explain, Don't Just Alert

A useful security result should help users understand the warning signs instead of simply displaying a risk label.

accessibility

Keep Security Accessible

We prioritize readable interfaces, simple language, and practical instructions so that digital safety is easier to understand.

Put Safety Before Convenience

We encourage verification before action, especially when money, credentials, OTPs, personal information, or account access are involved.

Keep Learning

Scam tactics evolve constantly, so our awareness content and detection approach are designed to grow with emerging threats.
