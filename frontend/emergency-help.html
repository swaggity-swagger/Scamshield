I Already Paid — What Should I Do Now?

Take a breath and act quickly. Follow these immediate steps to reduce further financial loss, secure your accounts, and preserve evidence.

favorite

Stay Calm. Focus on the Next Action.

Being scammed does not mean you failed. Fraudsters deliberately create pressure and confusion. Focus on stopping further contact, securing your accounts, and reporting the transaction quickly.

front_hand

1. Stop Communication

Stop communicating with the suspected scammer. Do not send additional money or respond to requests for "processing", "release", "verification", or "refund" fees. Block the sender after preserving important evidence.

Immediate Action arrow_forward
account_balance

2. Contact Your Bank or Payment Provider

Contact your bank, card issuer, wallet provider, or payment service using an official phone number or app. Explain that the transaction was fraudulent and ask what immediate account or transaction protections are available.

Act Quickly arrow_forward
lock_reset

3. Secure Your Accounts

Change passwords for affected accounts, beginning with your primary email. Sign out unknown sessions and enable multi-factor authentication where available. Never reuse a compromised password.

Prevent Further Access arrow_forward
save

4. Preserve Evidence

Save screenshots, transaction receipts, UTR or reference numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, URLs, usernames, and relevant chat messages before deleting or blocking anything.

Preserve Proof arrow_forward
policy

5. Report the Fraud

Report the fraudulent transaction through the appropriate official channel. In India, you can use the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal and the cyber-fraud helpline 1930. Keep your transaction details and evidence ready.

Official Reporting arrow_forward

Report a Cyber Fraud Incident

In India, you can report cyber fraud through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal or by calling the cyber-fraud helpline.

Do Not Pay More

Scammers may claim that an additional payment is required to "reverse", "unlock", or "recover" your money. Treat unexpected recovery-fee demands as suspicious and verify independently.

verified_user

Use Official Channels

Contact banks, payment providers, and government services through numbers and websites you find independently—not through links or phone numbers supplied by the suspected scammer.
